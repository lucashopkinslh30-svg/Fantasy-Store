Order

  1. Add products to your cart first (open a product and choose its size and color).
  2. Enter your full name.
  3. Write your complete delivery address: house number, street and city.
  4. Enter a phone number with 7 to 15 digits so we can reach you.
  5. Choose how you will pay, then press Place order. WhatsApp will open with your order ready: press Send to confirm it with us.
